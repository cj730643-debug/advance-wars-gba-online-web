All project additions are offered under MPL-2.0. Upstream modifications are identified in
`docs/SOURCE_AUDIT.md` and `patches/windows-portability.patch`; original copyright notices
are preserved. The upstream ROM fixtures are purpose-built CC0 diagnostic homebrew,
not either commercial Advance Wars game.

**How to obtain corresponding source:** every portable/installed distribution contains
`AdvanceWars-GBA-Online-Source.zip`. Unzip it to obtain the full modified core source,
desktop and server sources, build/packaging scripts, tests, notices and upstream pin.
This offline source copy accompanies the executable and is not dependent on a future
GitHub repository becoming available. Source ZIP excludes upstream cinema recordings
and commercial cartridge images; it retains the open diagnostic fixtures needed for
the focused unit tests. No commercial ROM, commercial BIOS or original game assets
are distributed.
